Privacy Policy

App: YouTube Music - Moataz (an unofficial personal YouTube Music client for LG webOS TVs)
Developer: Moataz Younes
Last updated: October 9, 2026

This app is a personal project and is not affiliated with YouTube or Google. This policy explains what data the app handles. Back to the app homepage.

No app-operated backend

The app runs entirely on your TV. The developer does not operate any server for it, and the developer does not receive, collect, store or sell any of your data. The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

Data the app uses

  • Catalog and playback: the TV fetches search results, catalog data and media streams directly from YouTube.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose to sign in, the app uses Google's OAuth device flow. You approve access on a Google page, not inside the app.
  • Library access (optional): after sign-in, the app can read and browse your liked videos and your saved YouTube Music playlists, albums and songs, so they can be shown on the TV. The app never edits your library.
  • Refresh token: the OAuth refresh token is stored only in the TV's local storage (localStorage). It is not sent to the developer or to any third party other than Google, to whom it is presented to obtain access.
  • Local data: local likes, play counts and resume positions are stored only on the TV.

Google and YouTube

Because the app talks directly to YouTube and Google, those services receive the requests the app makes (for example, your IP address, device information and the content you request or play), and your Google account data is handled under their own terms and policies. See the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service. You can review and revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Your controls

  • Unlink: choosing Unlink in the app revokes the token with Google and removes it from the TV.
  • Local data: clearing the app's data or uninstalling it from the TV removes the locally stored likes, play counts and resume positions.

Security

Data stays on your device, and connections to Google and YouTube use HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, and the developer cannot guarantee absolute security. Anyone with physical access to the TV and app could access the locally stored data, so unlink your account before giving the TV away.

Children

The app is not directed at children.
