YouTube Music - Moataz

YouTube Music - Moataz is a personal, unofficial YouTube Music client for LG webOS TVs. It is built and maintained by one person, Moataz Younes, for his own use. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by YouTube or Google. YouTube and YouTube Music are trademarks of Google LLC.

What the app does

  • Browses and plays music and videos from YouTube on the TV.
  • Lets you sign in with your Google account using Google's OAuth device flow (you enter a short code on your phone or computer).
  • After sign-in, it can optionally read and browse your liked videos and the YouTube playlists you own. Saved YouTube Music albums and songs are currently unavailable, because YouTube does not allow OAuth access to them.
  • Only after you explicitly confirm in the app each time, it can (a) rate a song as liked on your YouTube account, and (b) create private playlists and add songs to playlists you own.
  • It never deletes or edits playlists, never removes songs from playlists, and never removes a like on YouTube. Removing a like locally on the TV does not remove the rating on YouTube.
  • Keeps local likes, play counts and resume positions on the TV only.
  • Lets you unlink your account at any time from the app. Unlinking revokes the access token with Google and removes it from the TV.

How it works

The app runs on the TV and fetches catalog and playback data directly from YouTube. There is no server operated for this app, no advertising and no analytics.
